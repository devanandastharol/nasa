    What crops are selling for now

    Wholesale (modal) mandi prices in rupees per quintal, from 4 to 13 November 2026. 1 quintal = 100 kg.

    Rabi sowing is far behind last year

    Farmers had sown 37.56 lakh hectares by 30 October, against 69.02 lakh hectares at the same point last year. Wheat has only just begun at 0.19 lakh hectares. Sowing usually picks up through November, so the gap may narrow.

    Dry soil is making farmers wait
